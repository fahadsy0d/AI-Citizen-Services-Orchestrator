7. COMPETITIVE INSIGHTS TO INCORPORATE
==================================================

The design should specifically solve usability issues identified while reviewing government-service ecosystems.

DATA.GOV.IN OBSERVATION:
Scheme/dataset naming can be difficult for ordinary citizens to understand and descriptions may not immediately communicate what a scheme is actually for.

DESIGN RESPONSE:
- Explain schemes in plain language.
- Show “What is this for?”
- Show “Who might benefit?”
- Show “Why this appeared for you?”
- Use intent-based discovery instead of requiring exact scheme names.

Do NOT use popularity/adoption numbers as proof that a scheme is authentic.
If usage numbers are displayed, label them as contextual information only.

UMANG OBSERVATION:
Large amounts of secondary information or additional service promotion can distract users from their current task.

DESIGN RESPONSE:
- Task-first UI.
- Keep the current citizen goal dominant.
- Avoid unnecessary promotional content.
- Do not overwhelm the user with unrelated services.

SERVICEPLUS OBSERVATION:
Important service functionality can be visually buried beneath other information, and perceived loading/buffering can negatively affect usability.

DESIGN RESPONSE:
- Primary action above the fold.
- Clear visual hierarchy.
- Lightweight interface.
- Skeleton/loading states.
- Avoid unnecessary content.
- Make search/discovery obvious.

MYSCHEME OBSERVATION:
Users may need to know what they are looking for before searching, and statutory/bureaucratic language can be difficult to understand. External redirects can also break continuity.

DESIGN RESPONSE:
- Start from the user's problem.
- Translate bureaucratic terminology into plain language.
- Preserve a continuous journey.
- When an official external portal is required, clearly explain:
  “You are being redirected to the official government portal.”
- Provide a return/continuation concept when technically possible.

INDIA.GOV.IN OBSERVATION:
Large amounts of information can compete with important transactional services, while exact terminology can make discovery difficult.

DESIGN RESPONSE:
- Focus the home screen around the user's current task.
- Use semantic/intent-based search.
- Separate informational content from transactional actions.

DIGILOCKER OBSERVATION:
External integrations can experience delays, failures, or document-matching issues.

DESIGN RESPONSE:
Design resilient states:
- Loading
- Verification pending
- Retry
- Unable to connect
- Document mismatch
- Manual verification
- Official portal handoff

Never make the entire journey fail simply because one external service is temporarily unavailable.
